National CVD Summit 2026  •  Goa

Coming Soon

The National CVD Summit 2026, organised by Tricog in collaboration with the Government of Goa, is a national platform bringing together government leaders, cardiologists, clinicians, healthcare experts, public health professionals and technology stakeholders to address one of India’s most pressing health challenges; cardiovascular disease.

The Summit will focus on strengthening the entire continuum of cardiac care from prevention, screening and early detection to diagnosis, referral and timely treatment. It will explore how India can make cardiac care more accessible, take screening closer to patients, strengthen referral pathways and use technology to enable faster, more effective clinical decision-making.

A key focus will be Goa’s approach to strengthening cardiac care and the learnings that can be taken forward by other states. Through collaboration between government, clinical experts and technology partners, the Summit aims to move beyond discussion towards actionable recommendations, partnerships and frameworks that can translate into meaningful change.

The Summit will ultimately seek to build a stronger, more connected and accessible cardiac care ecosystem — helping India move from screening to action, and from early detection to timely care.
